# Murmur

Paper and ink. What others say sits on warm paper, what you say is set in indigo,
and vermilion means one thing only: new. The type sets the order of reading: names
first, then what was said, with days in small type between messages.

The decision I'd defend: the history knows what time it is. Scroll back through
six years of *Trail crew* and a chip names the date under your eye. If something
arrives while you're back there, the pill says who wrote and how it begins, so you
decide whether to leave your place.

A wide screen gets a sidebar and a centred reading column, not a stretched phone.

Built on Declare's own parts: virtualized lists, scroll anchoring, Popover,
Dialog, Audio. The one control I built is the voice scrubber, because none of them
offers it. The waveform drawn from the service's peaks is the track.
